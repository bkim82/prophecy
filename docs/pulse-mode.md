# pulse-mode

- Solo BTC arena route: `app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx`; multiplayer Pulse route: `app/duel/[market]/pulse/[matchId]/page.tsx`. Both are reached from the lobby mode switcher (`app/duel/page.tsx`).
- Multiplayer Pulse uses the shared match row and polling lifecycle. A 5-second pre-round countdown starts automatically after the second player joins; during the round each player can add server-priced long/short positions until their available cash is exhausted, with each open stake reserved until close, through `POST /api/match/[id]/action`; the timer deadline automatically closes remaining positions at the final price, releases their stakes, and realizes their P&L before comparing the players (`lib/match.ts`, `lib/pulse.ts`).
- Lobby wager: 50/100/200 Embers per side (`PULSE_WAGER_OPTIONS`, `lib/pulse.ts`), picked under Round length in `app/duel/page.tsx`, with a "You stake N → Winner takes 2N" payout card and round note; open-match rows and the queue panel show the wager. The room shows "N each · winner takes 2N" under the title, and `ArenaResultCard`'s optional `wager` prop renders the payout chip (+2N took the pot / −N wager lost / N refunded); practice omits it. Payout itself is `settleFundsIfNeeded` (`lib/match.ts`).
- Multiplayer entries/closes are priced by `getSpotPrice` (Coinbase Exchange ticker first, the same tape the chart plots); the entry marker is drawn at the server fill returned by the action, not the local tick (`app/duel/[market]/pulse/[matchId]/page.tsx:331-335`).
- Multiplayer stake: `PulseStakeStepper` (`app/PulseStakeStepper.tsx`, shared with `app/PulseMiniDock.tsx`) — typed amount, ±$25 (`PULSE_STAKE_STEP`), All in; defaults to $0 and is clamped to [0, available cash floored to the cent] (`lib/pulse.ts:32-36`) whenever nothing is open, so after a close it stays pre-loaded like practice. Editable during the 5s pre-round countdown. No fixed max: the server only checks stake > 0 and ≤ available cash (`app/api/match/[id]/action/route.ts:62-67`).
- Multiplayer liquidation (isolated margin): P&L is floored at −stake (`lib/pulse.ts:50-56`); liquidation price = entry × (1 ∓ 1/leverage) (`:43-47`). `liquidateIfDue` (`lib/match.ts:106`) scans the last 100 exchange trades (`getRecentTrades`, `lib/spotPrice.ts`) for any trade between a position's `openedAt` and the round deadline that touched its liquidation price, and closes it at exactly −stake; it runs on every poll before the bot and settlement (`app/api/match/[id]/route.ts:49`) and before every action (`action/route.ts:47`). The room shows a "Liquidated" card state, a notice + exit marker at the liquidation price for positions that vanish without a local close (`app/duel/[market]/pulse/[matchId]/page.tsx:238-255`), and once cash is under a cent with nothing open (`pulseIsOut`) the player is out for the rest of the round (`:204`).
- State machine: `setup` before entry (also re-entered immediately after a close, pre-loaded with the prior stake/leverage), `open` while a directional position is live, `settling` during final-price fetch, `result` after the 60-second round (`app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:48`, `:256-270`).
- Round starts on the first Long/Short press; duration is 60 seconds; starting bankroll is $100 (`app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:23-24`, `:256-270`).
- Bankroll persists across rounds: `settle()` commits each round's P&L into `bankroll` (floored at 0), and Rematch carries it forward — only a busted ($0) bankroll resets to $100 (`app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:181-252`, `:369-397`). Stake entry is clamped to `bankroll + realizedPnl` so a player can never bet more than they actually have, and stake presets/"Max" disable above that amount (`:467-476`, `:711`).
- Both Pulse routes render `MatchScreen` (`app/PulseMatch.tsx:675`) — pure presentation; each page owns its game state and passes numbers in (solo `app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:531`, room `app/duel/[market]/pulse/[matchId]/page.tsx:484`). One full-height column (100dvh, minus the app header on desktop), no scroll at 375/390px: top bar → scoreboard → speech bubble → `PulseMatchChart` (flexes) → controls; ≥1024px it goes two-column (`@media (min-width: 1024px)` grid in `app/globals.css`: chart left, `.pm-side` (scoreboard + bubble; `display: contents` on phones) top of a 400px right column, controls at its bottom; the chart sizes to that column (min `min(460px, viewport − 160px)`) instead of the full height, and the block is vertically centred under the pinned top bar; result screen stays the centred column); settled → full-screen `ResultView` (`app/PulseMatch.tsx:468`). Room specifics: controls state `waiting` during `open`/`predict` (Leave match in the footer), `settling` while the timer reads 0, `setup`/`open` otherwise (`page.tsx:411`); the amount chip opens `PulseStakeStepper`; the opponent's side stays hidden mid-round (`:514`) and their markers stay off the chart until `settled`; the bubble is the room's event line with no speaker. Reverse = close then enter the opposite side at the same stake (clamped)/leverage, one ◇ carrying the closed leg's P&L. Exit markers use the server fill recovered from realized P&L (`exitPriceFor`). Opponent identity stays "Opponent" until settle (only the poll route fills `opponentName`/`opponentHandle`). Positions stacked from `PulseMiniDock` show as "+N more open"; Close/Flip act on the newest.
- Controls (`Controls`, `app/PulseMatch.tsx:289`): before entry, Amount + Leverage chips (48px, open a menu upward; leverage tinted orange, each option shows its liq distance), Long/Short (72px, 3D bottom shadow that compresses on press, enter on pointerdown), helper "Enters at the live price · liquidation at ±(100/leverage)%" unless a page `hint` overrides it. <1024px (`app/globals.css` "Phones/tablets: no \"price goes up/down\""): no "price goes up/down" sub-label, generic helper hidden (`.pm-helper.is-default`; a page `hint` still shows), chips 38px, Long/Short 56px, top-bar icon buttons 36px / market pill 28px — the chart takes the freed height. In a position: "Close & lock it" (76px, live P&L at 26px; green + glow pulse when up, red when down), "Flip to Short/Long" (= Reverse) and a liq chip "Liq X · safe/close!/hit". Solo amount menu = $10/$25/$50/Max.
- Liquidation is never hidden: within `LIQ_NEAR_SHARE` (25%) of the entry→liq distance on the losing side (`app/PulseMatch.tsx:29`, `isNearLiq` `:408`) the chip turns red, `PulseMatchChart` draws a red dashed LIQ line (widening its y-range to include it), and `.pm-edge` pulses the screen edges red. The final 10s does the same edge glow, turns the timer ring red with a once-per-second heartbeat, and plays a `tick` sound when sound is on.
- Directional P&L = stake × price move percentage × position leverage, floored at −stake (isolated margin, like matches); Long benefits from an increase and Short from a decrease (`app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:86`). Solo/practice liquidates: a position worth −stake closes at its liquidation price with a notice + Sibyl line (`:336`). The solo winner compares round P&L (yours vs Sibyl's), not equity, so a carried-over bankroll can't decide it (`:424`).
- Close realizes the current position P&L and returns straight to the setup dock (same stake/leverage) so the next side can be picked immediately; Reverse realizes it and opens the opposite side at the same live price (`app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:305-367`). Both briefly lock (`isActing`, ~240ms) to prevent a rapid double-press from firing twice.
- Scoreboard (`app/PulseMatch.tsx:675`): avatars + "YOU"/"YOU · LONG" and "SIBYL · AI"/"SIBYL · SHORT" (or "OPPONENT") + balance (20px mono), a 68px draining `TimerRing` (`:161`; ring + the screen's top radial glow in `--market-accent`), header back arrow (`.pm-back`, `app/PulseMatch.tsx:787`) always shown, phones included, your live P&L (42px, `RollingNumber` digits `:97`; grey at $0, green + glow up, red down) with a % chip (P&L ÷ round-start bankroll), a lead line ("Dead even — make your call" / "You lead by $X" / "Sibyl leads by $X"), and a 12px tug-of-war split by lead ÷ largest lead this round with a spark at the boundary; a lead flip past ±$0.50 (`FLIP_THRESHOLD`, `:26`) shakes it and posts an aria-live announcement. Card border/background tint green when you lead, red when you trail (cent threshold, `EVEN_THRESHOLD`). Sibyl's face (`SibylFace`, `:132`): neutral before the round / when even, smug when she leads, frown when she trails. Sibyl's equity freezes at settle (`rivalFinalPnl`).
- Sibyl's own lifecycle is tracked as real chart trade data, not just a floating P&L: a `beginRound` call (`:272-281`) adds an `owner: "sibyl"` entry marker at the same price/time the player's round starts, and `settle()` adds a matching `owner: "sibyl"` exit marker at the frozen final price — no activity is simulated beyond what the existing rival-P&L model already computed (see "Chart markers" below).
- Sibyl's speech bubble (14px, tail up toward her corner) shows `sayLine` lines from the solo page's banks (`app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:33-44`), fired only by real events: round start (first entry), later entries, lead flips, a single tick moving your position ≥5% of stake (≥$1, throttled 8s, `:359`), liquidation, the final 10s and the result; the result line becomes the result screen's rematch taunt.
- No separate price row: `PulseMatchChart` shows the live price (30px mono, ▲/▼ coloured by the last tick) in its top-left corner. See [chart.md](chart.md) "Match chart".
- A loud signed percentage alert appears when the player total moves by at least 5% from the last alert, with a brief screen shake for emphasis; this is shared by solo and multiplayer Pulse (`app/PulseMovementAlert.tsx`, both Pulse page routes).
- Match chart markers: your entries ○ and exits □ (side colour); a rival's entries dashed. The `PriceChart` marker/toast vocabulary is now only used by the tutorial's live round.
- Feedback (`usePulseFeedback`, `app/PulseArenaParts.tsx`): `navigator.vibrate` (feature-detected) on entry/exit/reverse/win; Web Audio blips for those plus a three-note `win` chime and the final-10s `tick`, only once the top bar's sound button is on (muted by default, localStorage `pulse-sound-muted`).
- Settlement uses the freshest live tick with `/api/price` fallback; final chart point is frozen. Solo and multiplayer positions are closed at that final price and the resulting balance is carried into the result (`app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:181-252`, `lib/match.ts:163-180`).
- Styling: `.pm-*` in `app/globals.css` ("Pulse match screen", `:2077`). Colours follow the active theme: every `--pm-*` token derives from the app tokens (`--bg`, `--surface`, `--positive`, `--negative`, `--ember`, `--violet`, …), so embedded pieces (stake stepper, rematch check, movement alert) match too; `[data-theme="obsidian"] .pm-screen` pins the original palette (bg #0B0A10, cards #131219, green #3FD68A, red #F0606E, orange #F5A55A, purple #9B7BF7) and `[data-theme="light"] .pm-screen` darkens the brights, puts white type on the trade buttons and uses the lobby's Moonstone CTA purple. `PulseMatchChart`'s `C` colours are `var(--pm-*)` strings. `body:has(.pm-screen)` hides the app header and mobile bottom nav on phones (≤768px) only; on desktop the app header (logo, nav — none in beta mode — theme toggle, wallet/balance, account) stays in the app's own theme and the screen sizes to `100dvh - --pm-chrome` (65px header). Numbers use JetBrains Mono (`--font-arena-mono`, weights 400–800 in `app/layout.tsx`), "YOU WIN" Space Grotesk. Every animation is disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`; padding respects safe-area insets.
- Top bar (one row): 44px back button → `/duel`, market pill with live dot, mode tag ("PRACTICE · PULSE" / "ARENA · PULSE" / room "PULSE · 🔥2N POT"), win-streak badge (hidden at 0) and a 44px sound toggle. Win streaks are per browser: `useWinStreak` (`app/PulseMatch.tsx:55`) in localStorage `pulse-streak-practice`/`-solo`/`-match`, counted once per round id (solo `roundStart`, room `matchId`), reset by any non-win.
- Market identity: `.pm-screen` carries `data-market`, so the top-bar pill takes the market colour (NVDA green, BTC orange, ETH blue).
- Result screen (`ResultView`, `app/PulseMatch.tsx:468`): win = slow light rays, confetti, crown, "YOU WIN" stamp, hero counting up from 0 in orange (practice: round P&L, "practice payout · beat Sibyl by $X"; room: +2×wager Embers); loss = "<RIVAL> WINS", hero = your final P&L in red, plus a "what happened" line (your liquidation, else the rival's entry → final price; solo `page.tsx:498`, room `page.tsx:445`); tie = "DRAW". Streak badge at ≥2. Card: you vs rival with side + leverage + final P&L, plus Best tick (largest single P&L gain while holding), Held (time in positions), Entry → exit (last closed leg). Buttons: Rematch (64px purple 3D; practice = `playAgain`, challenge = `RematchButton` + `RematchCheck`, else "Play again" → `/duel`), Share win (`navigator.share`, else clipboard), "Play for real" → `/duel` in practice, else "Back to lobby". `ArenaResultCard` now serves only the tutorial.
- `app/duel/btc/pulse/trading.ts` holds pure spot buy/sell portfolio accounting with full-position clamping. Not yet wired to the page, which tracks a single leveraged directional position instead — see `docs/roadmap.md`.
