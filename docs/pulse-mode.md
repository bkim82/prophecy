# pulse-mode

- Solo BTC arena route: `app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx`; multiplayer Pulse route: `app/duel/[market]/pulse/[matchId]/page.tsx`. Both are reached from the lobby mode switcher (`app/duel/page.tsx`).
- Multiplayer Pulse uses the shared match row and polling lifecycle. A 5-second pre-round countdown starts automatically after the second player joins; during the round each player can add server-priced long/short positions until their available cash is exhausted, with each open stake reserved until close, through `POST /api/match/[id]/action`; the timer deadline automatically closes remaining positions at the final price, releases their stakes, and realizes their P&L before comparing the players (`lib/match.ts`, `lib/pulse.ts`).
- Lobby wager: 50/100/200 Embers per side (`PULSE_WAGER_OPTIONS`, `lib/pulse.ts`), picked next to Timer in `app/duel/page.tsx` with a "Winner takes 2×" line; open-match rows and the queue panel show the wager. The room shows "N each · winner takes 2N" under the title, and `ArenaResultCard`'s optional `wager` prop renders the payout chip (+2N took the pot / −N wager lost / N refunded); practice omits it. Payout itself is `settleFundsIfNeeded` (`lib/match.ts`).
- Multiplayer entries/closes are priced by `getSpotPrice` (Coinbase Exchange ticker first, the same tape the chart plots); the entry marker is drawn at the server fill returned by the action, not the local tick (`app/duel/[market]/pulse/[matchId]/page.tsx:331-335`).
- Multiplayer stake: `PulseStakeStepper` (`app/PulseStakeStepper.tsx`, shared with `app/PulseMiniDock.tsx`) — typed amount, ±$25 (`PULSE_STAKE_STEP`), All in; defaults to $0 and is clamped to [0, available cash floored to the cent] (`lib/pulse.ts:32-36`) whenever nothing is open, so after a close it stays pre-loaded like practice. Editable during the 5s pre-round countdown. No fixed max: the server only checks stake > 0 and ≤ available cash (`app/api/match/[id]/action/route.ts:62-67`).
- Multiplayer liquidation (isolated margin): P&L is floored at −stake (`lib/pulse.ts:50-56`); liquidation price = entry × (1 ∓ 1/leverage) (`:43-47`). `liquidateIfDue` (`lib/match.ts:106`) scans the last 100 exchange trades (`getRecentTrades`, `lib/spotPrice.ts`) for any trade between a position's `openedAt` and the round deadline that touched its liquidation price, and closes it at exactly −stake; it runs on every poll before the bot and settlement (`app/api/match/[id]/route.ts:49`) and before every action (`action/route.ts:47`). The room shows a "Liquidated" card state, a notice + exit marker at the liquidation price for positions that vanish without a local close (`app/duel/[market]/pulse/[matchId]/page.tsx:238-255`), and once cash is under a cent with nothing open (`pulseIsOut`) the player is out for the rest of the round (`:204`).
- State machine: `setup` before entry (also re-entered immediately after a close, pre-loaded with the prior stake/leverage), `open` while a directional position is live, `settling` during final-price fetch, `result` after the 60-second round (`app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:47`, `:293-307`).
- Round starts on the first Long/Short press; duration is 60 seconds; starting bankroll is $100 (`app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:22-23`, `:293-307`).
- Bankroll persists across rounds: `settle()` commits each round's P&L into `bankroll` (floored at 0), and Play Again carries it forward — only a busted ($0) bankroll resets to $100 (`app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:218-289`, `:406-434`). Stake entry is clamped to `bankroll + realizedPnl` so a player can never bet more than they actually have, and stake presets/"Max" disable above that amount (`:504-513`, `:748`).
- Multiplayer room is a copy of the practice UI (`app/duel/[market]/pulse/[matchId]/page.tsx`): same scoreboard (avatars, `CountdownRing` — 5s "Starting in" during `predict` — `LeadBar`, and a `.sibyl-bubble` line fed only by real events: round live, opponent entries/closes, lead flips, final 10s, result; `:208-280`), flashing headline, chart legend + `SoundToggle`, and `RoomDock` (`:516`) — practice's `TradingDock` with the stake stepper in place of the Amount dropdown (`.dock-row-wrap`/`.dock-stake` in `app/globals.css`). Like practice the room trades one position at a time: setup (stake/leverage/gear row, Long/Short, "Last: … closed" note) → open (chip/live P&L/meta row, Close + Reverse; hint shows the liquidation price; `:614`) → `settling` while the timer reads 0 → result (`ArenaResultCard`, `:642`). Reverse = close then enter the opposite side at the same stake (clamped to cash)/leverage, drawn as one ◇ carrying the closed leg's P&L (`:351`). Exit markers use the server fill recovered from realized P&L (`exitPriceFor`, `:58`). Opponent positions become dashed `owner: "room"` markers (`:257-280`), and at settle every still-open position (yours and theirs) gets an exit marker at the final price (`:282-293`). Opponent identity stays "Opponent" until settle: only the poll route fills `MatchView.opponentName` (via `opponentNames`, `app/api/match/[id]/route.ts`), and the room then shows it on the scoreboard, avatar initial, lead bar and result card, linked to `OPPONENT_PROFILE_HREF` (`/profile/zane_lfg` placeholder for every opponent). Positions stacked from `PulseMiniDock` show as "+N more open"; Close/Reverse act on the newest. Available cash after open-stake reservations drives the stepper; the scoreboard shows live equity, and at the deadline all positions close so the final balance includes their P&L (`:203`, `:196`, `lib/match.ts:163-180`).
- Solo trading dock (`TradingDock`, `app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:688`) is a compact two-row `.dock-bar` (no header/balance — the scoreboard above already shows that), not an `arena-panel`. Setup phase: row 1 is an Amount `<details>` dropdown (presets + "Max" = current available cash) and a Leverage `<details>` dropdown (100×/1000×/10000×, default 100×) plus a decorative gear glyph, row 2 is equal-width Long/Short (`:735-818`); stake and leverage lock into the opened position. A closed position leaves a small "Last: side closed @ price · P&L" note above the rows (`.dock-last-note`).
- Open-position dock (`:841-889`) collapses to the same two-row footprint: row 1 is a direction chip, the live P&L (`.dock-pnl-hero`/`.dock-live-pnl`), and an entry→current/size meta column; row 2 is Close (tinted by the position's side) and a secondary outlined Reverse.
- Directional P&L = stake × price move percentage × position leverage; Long benefits from an increase and Short from a decrease (`app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:93-99`).
- Close realizes the current position P&L and returns straight to the setup dock (same stake/leverage) so the next side can be picked immediately; Reverse realizes it and opens the opposite side at the same live price (`app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:342-404`). Both briefly lock (`isActing`, ~240ms) to prevent a rapid double-press from firing twice.
- Scoreboard shows player and lightweight local AI rival ("Sibyl") equity/P&L via avatars (`.duel-avatar`, initials-on-gradient for you, an inline SVG face for Sibyl, `SibylFace`, `app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:108-144`) either side of a circular SVG countdown (`CountdownRing`, `app/PulseArenaParts.tsx`, shared with the multiplayer room, pulsing under 10s via `.duel-ring-progress.is-urgent`). A `LeadBar` (`app/PulseArenaParts.tsx`, `rivalName` prop) below shows "You lead by $X"/"Sibyl leads by $X"/"Tied", scaled against the largest lead seen so far this round (`maxLeadRef`, `app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:460`); a lead-change pops the leading avatar (`.duel-avatar.is-lead`) once per flip. Sibyl's equity/P&L freezes at settlement (`rivalFinalPnl`, set in `settle()`, `:218-289`) rather than continuing to track the live price during the result screen.
- Sibyl's own lifecycle is tracked as real chart trade data, not just a floating P&L: a `beginRound` call (`:309-318`) adds an `owner: "sibyl"` entry marker at the same price/time the player's round starts, and `settle()` adds a matching `owner: "sibyl"` exit marker at the frozen final price — no activity is simulated beyond what the existing rival-P&L model already computed (see "Chart markers" below).
- Sibyl also sends short contextual lines (`sayLine`, `app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:196-199`; line variants `:37-44`; triggers at round start `:317`, a lead flip `:466-475`, the final-10s mark `:477-486`, and the round result `:488-493`) grounded only in real state transitions — never invented trade commentary.
- The headline price animates per-changed-character (`FlashingPrice`, `app/PulseArenaParts.tsx`, used by both Pulse routes) rather than flashing the whole string, and the heading size was reduced (`text-4xl`).
- A loud signed percentage alert appears when the player total moves by at least 5% from the last alert, with a brief screen shake for emphasis; this is shared by solo and multiplayer Pulse (`app/PulseMovementAlert.tsx`, both Pulse page routes).
- Chart markers distinguish directional entry circles, exit squares, and reversal diamonds, each with a direction-coloured drop-shadow; an optional `owner: "you" | "sibyl" | "room"` on `TradeMarker` (`app/PriceChart.tsx:14-24`) renders any non-"you" markers (Sibyl, Oracle Room players) with a dashed outline and slightly reduced opacity instead of a second colour (`:620-660`). While a position is open, an optional `openEntry` prop draws a labeled dashed line at its entry price out to the live head (`:586-618`); the newest closing trade (with a `pnl`) gets a transient fading P&L callout rather than every historical exit (`:662-686`).
- Haptic vibration is unconditional on entry, exit, and reverse; the Web Audio tick is muted by default (localStorage `pulse-sound-muted`, `MUTE_STORAGE_KEY`) and only plays once the player toggles the sound control under the chart on (`usePulseFeedback` + `SoundToggle`, `app/PulseArenaParts.tsx`, used by both Pulse routes).
- Settlement uses the freshest live tick with `/api/price` fallback; final chart point is frozen. Solo and multiplayer positions are closed at that final price and the resulting balance is carried into the result (`app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:218-289`, `lib/match.ts:163-180`).
- Styling goes through the shared tokens, not literals: long/short = `--chart-up`/`--chart-down`, P&L sign = `--positive`/`--negative`, panels = `.panel`-equivalent `--surface`/`--line` (`app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:32-33`, `:101-103`). The global header and theme toggle come from `app/layout.tsx` — the page renders no chrome of its own.
- Both Pulse routes share a `.arena-shell` entrance (children stage-rise in, reusing the lobby's `stage-rise` keyframe) and a `.price-flash` headline on every whole-tick move (same cue as the lobby ticker). `.arena-panel` glow borders still apply to the scoreboard; both docks opted out of glow (`.dock-bar`, `.dock-action` in `app/globals.css`, "Solo Pulse trading dock" section) in favor of a flatter, compact bar (multiplayer additions in the "Multiplayer room … reuses the dock" block right after it). Both routes pulse the countdown ring via `.duel-ring-progress.is-urgent` (see above).
- Market identity: both routes set `data-market` on `.arena-shell` and render `PulseMarketTitle` (`app/PulseMarketTitle.tsx`) — coin glyph + ticker pill in `--market-accent`; inside `[data-market]`, `.arena-panel` border/top edge/corner glow, the `.price-chart` and solo `.arena-dock` outlines, and the "X / USD" label take the market colour (BTC orange, ETH blue, DOGE gold) instead of brand teal (`app/globals.css`, "Market identity in the arena"). Light theme darkens `--market-accent-strong` per market for contrast on light surfaces.
- Result card (`ArenaResultCard`, `app/PulseArenaParts.tsx`, used by both routes; multiplayer's Play Again links back to `/duel`): winner/tie computed by comparing final equities (never just the player's own P&L sign). Header = crossed-swords "Pulse duel" label, "Round won/lost/drawn", and "<name> finished $X ahead" (the equity gap; rival name links via `rivalHref` when given). Body = one versus scoreboard (`VersusSide`: avatar + name, Final balance, Round P&L coloured by sign; the winner gets a violet border + WINNER badge; practice passes Sibyl's face as `rivalAvatar`). No lead bar (no ranked rating exists to show instead). Takeaway = biggest |P&L| trade as "Largest win/loss: Side · P&L" + "Entered at …; exited at …." (`roundTakeaway`). "Review N trades" (collapsed) lists whole trades — Position badge, Entry → Exit, P&L ($0.00 flat in a neutral tone) — built by `roundTrades` from closing events' `closed` leg (exit/reverse/liquidation/settle all record it in both pages). Final price sits in a small footer. A win gets a brief CSS particle burst (`ResultBurst`, `app/PulseArenaParts.tsx`, disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`).
- `app/duel/btc/pulse/trading.ts` holds pure spot buy/sell portfolio accounting with full-position clamping. Not yet wired to the page, which tracks a single leveraged directional position instead — see `docs/roadmap.md`.
