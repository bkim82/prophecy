# pulse-mode

- Solo BTC arena route: `app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx`; multiplayer Pulse route: `app/duel/[market]/pulse/[matchId]/page.tsx`. Both are reached from the lobby mode switcher (`app/duel/page.tsx`).
- Multiplayer Pulse uses the shared match row and polling lifecycle. A 5-second pre-round countdown starts automatically after the second player joins; during the round each player can add server-priced long/short positions until their available cash is exhausted, with each open stake reserved until close, through `POST /api/match/[id]/action`; the timer deadline automatically closes remaining positions at the final price, releases their stakes, and realizes their P&L before comparing the players (`lib/match.ts`, `lib/pulse.ts`).
- State machine: `setup` before entry (also re-entered immediately after a close, pre-loaded with the prior stake/leverage), `open` while a directional position is live, `settling` during final-price fetch, `result` after the 60-second round (`app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:38`, `:459-475`).
- Round starts on the first Long/Short press; duration is 60 seconds; starting bankroll is $100 (`app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:9-10`, `:459-475`).
- Bankroll persists across rounds: `settle()` commits each round's P&L into `bankroll` (floored at 0), and Play Again carries it forward — only a busted ($0) bankroll resets to $100 (`app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:274`, `:386-451`, `:574-603`). Stake entry is clamped to `bankroll + realizedPnl` so a player can never bet more than they actually have, and stake presets/"Max" disable above that amount (`:694-696`, `:1022`).
- Multiplayer trading dock Balance shows available cash after realized P&L and open-stake reservations; the scoreboard continues to show live equity including open-position P&L, and at the timer deadline all positions are closed so the final balance includes their P&L (`app/duel/[market]/pulse/[matchId]/page.tsx:109`, `:161`, `lib/match.ts:163-180`).
- Solo trading dock (`TradingDock`, `app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:916`) is a compact two-row `.dock-bar` (no header/balance — the scoreboard above already shows that), not an `arena-panel`. Setup phase: row 1 is an Amount `<details>` dropdown (presets + "Max" = current available cash) and a Leverage `<details>` dropdown (100×/1000×/10000×, default 100×) plus a decorative gear glyph, row 2 is equal-width Long/Short (`:1013-1034`); stake and leverage lock into the opened position. A closed position leaves a small "Last: side closed @ price · P&L" note above the rows (`.dock-last-note`).
- Open-position dock (`:1070-1109`) collapses to the same two-row footprint: row 1 is a direction chip, the live P&L (`.dock-pnl-hero`/`.dock-live-pnl`), and an entry→current/size meta column; row 2 is Close (tinted by the position's side) and a secondary outlined Reverse.
- Directional P&L = stake × price move percentage × position leverage; Long benefits from an increase and Short from a decrease (`app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:86-92`).
- Close realizes the current position P&L and returns straight to the setup dock (same stake/leverage) so the next side can be picked immediately; Reverse realizes it and opens the opposite side at the same live price (`app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:510-572`). Both briefly lock (`isActing`, ~240ms) to prevent a rapid double-press from firing twice.
- Scoreboard shows player and lightweight local AI rival ("Sibyl") equity/P&L via avatars (`.duel-avatar`, initials-on-gradient for you, an inline SVG face for Sibyl, `SibylFace`, `app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:156-192`) either side of a circular SVG countdown (`CountdownRing`, `:125-154`, pulsing under 10s via `.duel-ring-progress.is-urgent`). A `LeadBar` (`:194-215`) below shows "You lead by $X"/"Sibyl leads by $X"/"Tied", scaled against the largest lead seen so far this round (`maxLeadRef`, `:629`); a lead-change pops the leading avatar (`.duel-avatar.is-lead`) once per flip. Sibyl's equity/P&L freezes at settlement (`rivalFinalPnl`, set in `settle()`, `:386-451`) rather than continuing to track the live price during the result screen.
- Sibyl's own lifecycle is tracked as real chart trade data, not just a floating P&L: a `beginRound` call (`:477-485`) adds an `owner: "sibyl"` entry marker at the same price/time the player's round starts, and `settle()` adds a matching `owner: "sibyl"` exit marker at the frozen final price — no activity is simulated beyond what the existing rival-P&L model already computed (see "Chart markers" below).
- Sibyl also sends short contextual lines (`sayLine`, `app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:322-325`; line variants `:28-36`; triggers at round start `:485`, a lead flip `:651-659`, the final-10s mark `:661-669`, and the round result `:671-676`) grounded only in real state transitions — never invented trade commentary.
- The headline price animates per-changed-character (`FlashingPrice`, mask diffed in the `priceFlash` effect) rather than flashing the whole string, and the heading size was reduced (`text-4xl`).
- A loud signed percentage alert appears when the player total moves by at least 5% from the last alert, with a brief screen shake for emphasis; this is shared by solo and multiplayer Pulse (`app/PulseMovementAlert.tsx`, both Pulse page routes).
- Chart markers distinguish directional entry circles, exit squares, and reversal diamonds, each with a direction-coloured drop-shadow; an optional `owner: "you" | "sibyl"` on `TradeMarker` (`app/PriceChart.tsx:14-23`) renders Sibyl's markers with a dashed outline and slightly reduced opacity instead of a second colour (`:620-660`). While a position is open, an optional `openEntry` prop draws a labeled dashed line at its entry price out to the live head (`:586-618`); the newest closing trade (with a `pnl`) gets a transient fading P&L callout rather than every historical exit (`:662-686`).
- Haptic vibration is unconditional on entry, exit, and reverse; the Web Audio tick is muted by default (localStorage `pulse-sound-muted`, `MUTE_STORAGE_KEY`) and only plays once the player toggles the sound control under the chart on (`playFeedback`, `app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:34-50`).
- Settlement uses the freshest live tick with `/api/price` fallback; final chart point is frozen. Solo and multiplayer positions are closed at that final price and the resulting balance is carried into the result (`app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:386-456`, `lib/match.ts:163-180`).
- Styling goes through the shared tokens, not literals: long/short = `--chart-up`/`--chart-down`, P&L sign = `--positive`/`--negative`, panels = `.panel`-equivalent `--surface`/`--line` (`app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:20-21`, `:94-96`). The global header and theme toggle come from `app/layout.tsx` — the page renders no chrome of its own.
- Both Pulse routes share a `.arena-shell` entrance (children stage-rise in, reusing the lobby's `stage-rise` keyframe) and a `.price-flash` headline on every whole-tick move (same cue as the lobby ticker). `.arena-panel` glow borders still apply to the scoreboard (and the multiplayer trading dock/`.arena-choice` glowing Long/Short CTAs, `app/duel/[market]/pulse/[matchId]/page.tsx:55`, `:147-152`, `:204`); the solo dock opted out of both (`.dock-bar`, `.dock-action` in `app/globals.css`, "Solo Pulse trading dock" section) in favor of a flatter, compact bar. The multiplayer route still pulses its plain-text timer via `.arena-timer.is-urgent`; the solo route replaced that with the countdown ring's own `.duel-ring-progress.is-urgent` pulse (see above).
- Market identity: both routes set `data-market` on `.arena-shell` and render `PulseMarketTitle` (`app/PulseMarketTitle.tsx`) — coin glyph + ticker pill in `--market-accent`; inside `[data-market]`, `.arena-panel` border/top edge/corner glow, the `.price-chart` and solo `.arena-dock` outlines, and the "X / USD" label take the market colour (BTC orange, ETH blue, DOGE gold) instead of brand teal (`app/globals.css`, "Market identity in the arena"). Light theme darkens `--market-accent-strong` per market for contrast on sand.
- Result card (solo only, `ResultCard`, `app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:1158-1248`): winner/tie computed by comparing final equities (never just the player's own P&L sign), both final balances, the reused `LeadBar`, a one-line takeaway built only from the player's own real trades this round (`roundTakeaway`, `:103-110`, "No trades this round…" when none were made), "Play Again", and a "Review trades" disclosure listing the round's trades. A win gets a brief CSS particle burst (`ResultBurst`, `:217-246`, disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`); a loss/tie just gets the card's own fade-in.
- `app/duel/btc/pulse/trading.ts` holds pure spot buy/sell portfolio accounting with full-position clamping. Not yet wired to the page, which tracks a single leveraged directional position instead — see `docs/roadmap.md`.
