# pulse-mode

- Solo BTC arena route: `app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx`; multiplayer Pulse route: `app/duel/[market]/pulse/[matchId]/page.tsx`. Both are reached from the lobby mode switcher (`app/duel/page.tsx`).
- Multiplayer Pulse uses the shared match row and polling lifecycle. A 5-second pre-round countdown starts automatically after the second player joins; during the round each player can add server-priced long/short positions until their available cash is exhausted, with each open stake reserved until close, through `POST /api/match/[id]/action`; the timer deadline automatically closes remaining positions at the final price, releases their stakes, and realizes their P&L before comparing the players (`lib/match.ts`, `lib/pulse.ts`).
- Lobby wager: 50/100/200 Embers per side (`PULSE_WAGER_OPTIONS`, `lib/pulse.ts`), picked under Round length in `app/duel/page.tsx`, with a "You stake N → Winner takes 2N" payout card and round note; open-match rows and the queue panel show the wager. The room shows "N each · winner takes 2N" under the title, and `ArenaResultCard`'s optional `wager` prop renders the payout chip (+2N took the pot / −N wager lost / N refunded); practice omits it. Payout itself is `settleFundsIfNeeded` (`lib/match.ts`).
- Multiplayer entries/closes are priced by `getSpotPrice` (Coinbase Exchange ticker first, the same tape the chart plots); the entry marker is drawn at the server fill returned by the action, not the local tick (`app/duel/[market]/pulse/[matchId]/page.tsx:331-335`).
- Multiplayer stake: the match screen's stake chip (`MatchStake`, below; `PulseStakeStepper`, `app/PulseStakeStepper.tsx`, now only serves `app/PulseMiniDock.tsx` and the tutorial's live round); defaults to $0 and is clamped to [0, available cash floored to the cent] (`lib/pulse.ts:32-36`) whenever nothing is open, so after a close it stays pre-loaded like practice. Editable during the 5s pre-round countdown. No fixed max: the server only checks stake > 0 and ≤ available cash (`app/api/match/[id]/action/route.ts:62-67`).
- Multiplayer liquidation (isolated margin): P&L is floored at −stake (`lib/pulse.ts:50-56`); liquidation price = entry × (1 ∓ 1/leverage) (`:43-47`). `liquidateIfDue` (`lib/match.ts:106`) scans the last 100 exchange trades (`getRecentTrades`, `lib/spotPrice.ts`) for any trade between a position's `openedAt` and the round deadline that touched its liquidation price, and closes it at exactly −stake; it runs on every poll before the bot and settlement (`app/api/match/[id]/route.ts:49`) and before every action (`action/route.ts:47`). The room shows a "Liquidated" card state, a notice + exit marker at the liquidation price for positions that vanish without a local close (`app/duel/[market]/pulse/[matchId]/page.tsx:238-255`), and once cash is under a cent with nothing open (`pulseIsOut`) the player is out for the rest of the round (`:204`).
- State machine: `setup` before entry (also re-entered immediately after a close, pre-loaded with the prior stake/leverage), `open` while a directional position is live, `settling` during final-price fetch, `result` after the 60-second round (`app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:48`, `:256-270`).
- Round starts on the first Long/Short press; duration is 60 seconds; starting bankroll is $100 (`app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:23-24`, `:256-270`).
- Bankroll persists across rounds: `settle()` commits each round's P&L into `bankroll` (floored at 0), and Rematch carries it forward — only a busted ($0) bankroll resets to $100 (`app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:181-252`, `:369-397`). Stake entry is clamped to `bankroll + realizedPnl` so a player can never bet more than they actually have, and stake presets/"Max" disable above that amount (`:467-476`, `:711`).
- Both Pulse routes render `MatchScreen` (`app/PulseMatch.tsx:746`) — pure presentation; each page owns its game state and passes numbers in (solo `app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:529`, room `app/duel/[market]/pulse/[matchId]/page.tsx:479`). One full-height column (100dvh, minus the app header on desktop), no scroll at 360–390px wide (checked down to 360×640): top bar → scoreboard → room event bubble (multiplayer) → `PulseMatchChart` (flexes) → controls + footnote; ≥1024px it stacks full-width up to 1280px (`@media (min-width: 1024px)` grid in `app/globals.css`: top bar, scoreboard strip, chart (min 300px, fills the rest), controls in one row (Stake 290px | Leverage 290px | Long | Short ≥1280px, four equal columns 1024–1279px), footnote under it; the bubble floats in the chart's top-right; result screen stays the centred column); settled → full-screen `ResultView` (`app/PulseMatch.tsx:526`). Room specifics: controls state `waiting` during `open`/`predict` (Leave match in the footer), `settling` while the timer reads 0, `setup`/`open` otherwise (`page.tsx:411`); the stake is the same typed `MatchStake` chip as solo; the opponent's side stays hidden mid-round (`:514`) and their markers stay off the chart until `settled`; the bubble (`bubble` prop) is the room's event line. Reverse = close then enter the opposite side at the same stake (clamped) and the Flip control's leverage (`reversePosition(position, nextLeverage)`), one ◇ carrying the closed leg's P&L. Exit markers use the server fill recovered from realized P&L (`exitPriceFor`). Opponent identity stays "Opponent" until settle (only the poll route fills `opponentName`/`opponentHandle`). Positions stacked from `PulseMiniDock` show as "+N more open"; Close/Flip act on the newest.
- Controls (`Controls`, `app/PulseMatch.tsx:348`): every chip and button shares `--pm-ctl` (56px <1024px, 52px under 740px tall, 100px desktop). Stake (`MatchStake`, `:174`): dark card, −25 | an inset chip labelled "Stake" holding a real text field (text cursor, a fake caret blinking after the number until focus; clicking the digits puts the caret there, clicking elsewhere in the field puts it after the number so you can keep typing; `inputMode="decimal"`, clamped via `clampPulseStake`; 28px mono desktop) | +25 (`PULSE_STAKE_STEP`). Leverage (`MatchLeverage`, `:262`, ends are `LeverageStep` `:243`, via `leverageNeighbor` `:237`): amber card (amber border, faint amber gradient), ÷10 | "Leverage" over "100×" in glowing amber over a 4-segment risk meter (`RISK_SEGMENTS` `:259`; lowest option lights 1, highest all 4; amber → orange → red) | ×10, stepping through the page's options (each 10× the last; labels are the actual ratio, ÷/× set in the sans face so they don't read as +/x; ÷ disabled (dimmed) at the lowest, × at the highest). Both modes start at 100× (solo `DEFAULT_LEVERAGE`, room `useState(100)`); options 100/1000/10000× (`PULSE_LEVERAGE_OPTIONS`, `lib/pulse.ts:16`, also the server's `isPulseLeverage`). Long/Short: solid #2EE59D / #FF5C7A buttons, 18px radius, dark type, a hard bottom edge (`--pm-drop` 4px <1024px, 6px desktop; #129B66 / #B8304B) plus a soft glow, sinking onto the edge when pressed; left: "Long"/"Short" (30px desktop) over the payout "+$100 per +1%" / "+$100 per −1%" (stake × leverage ÷ 100, `payoutText` `:163`), right: a trend-up/down stroke icon; enter on pointerdown. Footnote (`.pm-foot`): left "You’re out if it moves (100/leverage)% the wrong way." unless a page `hint` overrides it, right (mono) "Liquidation ≈ L long · S short" from the live price at the current leverage; the default footnote hides <1024px (`.pm-foot.is-default`), a hint always shows. In a position: "Close & lock it" solid green/red on the same hard edge (dark type; glow pulse when up), `FlipControl` (`:289`; = Reverse) — the wider 1.8fr of the row: a tab strip `100× 1000× 10000×` (the page's options; amber on the pick, `aria-pressed`) sitting on a button "Flip to Short at 100×" / "out at <liq>" (the new side's liquidation price from the live price at the picked leverage), tinted the side it flips to; the tab starts at 100× (`FLIP_BASE_LEVERAGE` `:282`) for every position, whatever it opened at; the button flips at the pick and makes it the next setup's leverage (`onLeverage` then `onFlip(leverage)`). Liq and Close line up with the button, the tabs poke up above (on desktop Flip + Liq and Close split the row half and half); and a liq chip "Liq X · safe/close!/hit" (<1024px the state stacks under the price so a BTC price fits).
- Liquidation is never hidden: within `LIQ_NEAR_SHARE` (25%) of the entry→liq distance on the losing side (`app/PulseMatch.tsx:33`, `isNearLiq` `:466`) the chip turns red, `PulseMatchChart` draws a red dashed LIQ line (widening its y-range to include it), and `.pm-edge` pulses the screen edges red. The countdown beeps — the number pops and glows once a second, ring and digits — yellow (`--pm-yellow`) from 15s and red from 5s, with a `tick` sound per beep when sound is on; the edge glow joins for the red 5s.
- Directional P&L = stake × price move percentage × position leverage, floored at −stake (isolated margin, like matches); Long benefits from an increase and Short from a decrease (`app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:86`). Solo/practice liquidates: a position worth −stake closes at its liquidation price with a notice + Sibyl line (`:336`). The solo winner compares round P&L (yours vs Sibyl's), not equity, so a carried-over bankroll can't decide it (`:424`).
- Close realizes the current position P&L and returns straight to the setup dock (same stake/leverage) so the next side can be picked immediately; Reverse realizes it and opens the opposite side at the same live price, at the Flip control's leverage (`reversePosition(nextLeverage)`) (`app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:305-367`). Both briefly lock (`isActing`, ~240ms) to prevent a rapid double-press from firing twice.
- Scoreboard (`app/PulseMatch.tsx:746`): your card | countdown | rival card (mirrored), then the tug-of-war. Cards: 18px radius (20px desktop), your violet gradient fading from the left with a 35% violet border, the rival's amber fading from the right, right-aligned. Players: identity disc (`Avatar` `:133`; you violet "Y", the rival amber `--pm-amber` with Sibyl's face) with a double ring (dark gap + coloured ring) and a glow (stronger on the leader); 34px beside the label on phones, 60px beside label/balance/status on desktop; label in small caps ("You" light violet; "Sibyl · AI"/"Sibyl · Short" or "Opponent" light amber), big balance (36px mono 800 desktop; on phones up to 28px, shrunk via inline `--pm-bal-em` so a long figure fits its card) and a status line: yours "No position yet" / "Long 100× from 181.10" / "Liquidated"; Sibyl's is her latest `sayLine` line (`rival.line`) in curly quotes (speech), else "Waiting for a clearer move" / her side; a room opponent's "Side hidden until the bell" until revealed. Centre: `Countdown` (`:144`), a ring that drains with the clock (`timer.total`, default 60; dark track, white glowing arc) around the seconds (44px desktop, 30px phones) over "seconds" (room pre-round: "to start"), 120px desktop / 84px phones (76px under 740px tall or ≤400px wide), each second dropping in. Tug-of-war: one 10px rounded track, violet gradient (#5A49E6 → #8B7BFF) meeting amber (#FFB547 → #E88A12) at your share (lead ÷ largest lead this round, 4–96%, inline `--pm-share`), each with a glow, each end's share as a % label; a pill rides the seam (clamped 56px from the ends): "DEAD EVEN", else the leader and their margin over 50% ("YOU +12%" / "SIBYL +12%"), with the dollar gap as screen-reader text. A lead flip past ±$0.50 (`FLIP_THRESHOLD`, `:30`) shakes it and posts an aria-live announcement. Header back arrow (`.pm-back`, `:857`) always shown, phones included. Sibyl's face (`SibylFace`, `:111`): neutral before the round / when even, smug when she leads, frown when she trails. Sibyl's equity freezes at settle (`rivalFinalPnl`).
- Sibyl's own lifecycle is tracked as real chart trade data, not just a floating P&L: a `beginRound` call (`:272-281`) adds an `owner: "sibyl"` entry marker at the same price/time the player's round starts, and `settle()` adds a matching `owner: "sibyl"` exit marker at the frozen final price — no activity is simulated beyond what the existing rival-P&L model already computed (see "Chart markers" below).
- Sibyl's status line (under her balance, fading in per line) shows `sayLine` lines from the solo page's banks (`app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:33-44`), fired only by real events: round start (first entry), later entries, lead flips, a single tick moving your position ≥5% of stake (≥$1, throttled 8s, `:359`), liquidation, the final 10s and the result; the result line becomes the result screen's rematch taunt (amber `.pm-bubble.is-sibyl`).
- No separate price row: `PulseMatchChart` shows the live price (40px white mono desktop, 30px phones) with the change since the window's first tick in a green/red badge in its top-left corner, and the window's H/L top-right. See [chart.md](chart.md) "Match chart".
- A loud signed percentage alert appears when the player total moves by at least 5% from the last alert, with a brief screen shake for emphasis; this is shared by solo and multiplayer Pulse (`app/PulseMovementAlert.tsx`, both Pulse page routes).
- Match chart markers: your entries ○ and exits □ (side colour); a rival's entries dashed. The `PriceChart` marker/toast vocabulary is now only used by the tutorial's live round.
- Feedback (`usePulseFeedback`, `app/PulseArenaParts.tsx`): `navigator.vibrate` (feature-detected) on entry/exit/reverse/win; Web Audio blips for those plus a three-note `win` chime and the countdown's `tick` (each second from 15s), only once the top bar's sound button is on (muted by default, localStorage `pulse-sound-muted`).
- Settlement uses the freshest live tick with `/api/price` fallback; final chart point is frozen. Solo and multiplayer positions are closed at that final price and the resulting balance is carried into the result (`app/duel/btc/pulse/page.tsx:181-252`, `lib/match.ts:163-180`).
